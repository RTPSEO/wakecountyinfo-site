Where the numbers come from

We start with free, public data published by Wake County and its towns. No guesses, no scraped listing sites.

Property records

Wake County Tax Administration real estate data files: ownership, assessed values and property details for every parcel.

Home sales

Wake County qualified sales: arm's-length sales reviewed by county appraisers. Foreclosures and related-party sales are excluded.

Building permits

Wake County and municipal building permit data, used to track new construction.

About this site

Wake County Info is an independent project. It is not affiliated with Wake County Government or any town. Real estate is the first topic; more Wake County topics will follow.
